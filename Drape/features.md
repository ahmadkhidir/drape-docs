# Features

## Overview

The Drape feature set establishes an experiential digital fabric atelier that unites tactile sensory exploration with generative AI virtual tailoring. It enables customers and couture designers to interact with physical textile properties, simulate bespoke garments directly onto their own bodies with authentic physical drape and lighting, and seamlessly transition from digital visualization to custom yardage cutting and master tailor fulfillment.

## Priority Definitions

| Priority | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **P0** | Must Have | Critical for launch. Core sensory catalog, WebGPU cloth sandbox, Neural Tailor try-on, biometric compliance, yardage estimation, and custom-cut checkout. |
| **P1** | Should Have | Important but not launch-blocking. 360° motion walk loops, tailor spec exports, "Send to Atelier" concierge, and tactile spatial audio. |
| **P2** | Could Have | Nice to have. Adds bespoke delight and physical connectivity. Generative dye-lab print customization and NFC/AR swatch projection. |
| **P3** | Won't Have Now | Acknowledged but deferred. Fully automated robotic sewing and consumer pattern drafting marketplaces. |
